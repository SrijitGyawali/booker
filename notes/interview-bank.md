# Interview Bank

Every interview question from the roadmap, answered **in my own words** and
practised **out loud**. Wherever possible the answer points at booker:
*"In booker, I…"*.

- Started on Day 0. Each day's questions are added here (step 4 of the daily routine).
- Weak answers get a ⚠️ and are re-answered on review days (7, 16, 28, 39, 45).
- Tightened on Day 52: every answer in my own words, every core topic at three levels.

Format for each entry:

```markdown
### <Question>?
<My answer, sized for the time I would have in an interview.>
**In booker:** <what I built or measured that proves it>
```
